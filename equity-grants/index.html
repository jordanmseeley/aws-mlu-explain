Foundation

Grant types set the tone

RSUs, stock options, and performance shares all deliver ownership differently. Choose formats that match your talent strategy, risk-tolerance, and maturity as a company.

Timing

Vesting turns potential into pay

A 1-year cliff rewards early retention, while monthly or quarterly vesting keeps momentum. Front-loaded schedules help new joiners, while back-loaded curves reward staying power.

Momentum

Refresh grants prevent stagnation

Annual refreshes based on performance keep total equity growing. Calibration policies and budget guardrails make outcomes predictable and fair across teams.

Moments of truth

Taxes and liquidity windows matter

With RSUs, taxes land at each vest. Option holders worry about strike prices and exercise windows. Clear guidance avoids surprises around blackout periods and lockups.

Communication

Storytelling builds trust

Pair compensation numbers with plain-language narratives. Show how equity grows with company value, how refresh cycles work, and what protections exist if markets swing.

Design patterns

Mix and match elements to fit your compensation philosophy

Each element below can be combined. For example, pair a 25/25/25/25 vest with an annual refresh and sign-on grant to balance attraction and retention.

Grant formats

  • Time-based RSUs for simplicity
  • Stock options for upside alignment
  • Performance shares tied to milestones

Vesting recipes

  • Standard: 1-year cliff, monthly vest
  • Front-loaded: 40/20/20/20 for quick impact
  • Long-term: 10/20/30/40 to reward tenure

Refresh policy

  • Annual performance-based refresh
  • Promotion triggers with spot grants
  • Equity true-ups for market adjustments

Risk guardrails

  • Blackout calendars and trading windows
  • Education on tax withholding and 83(b)
  • Clear post-termination exercise rules

Lifecycle

What an employee experiences over four years

Offer

Initial grant

Mix of base RSUs and a sign-on to bridge the cliff year. Provide modeled value at target stock price and downside scenarios.

Month 12

Cliff vest + first refresh

A meaningful first vest builds trust. A refresh grant extends the runway and signals continued investment in the employee.

Year 2-3

Momentum and mobility

Promotions and stretch roles trigger spot grants. Regular vesting keeps realized value steady, even in choppy markets.

Year 4

Retention moment

Graduated refreshes avoid a value cliff and reduce pressure to renegotiate. Options to extend exercise windows support alumni.

Turn this story into a plan

Start with a simple RSU program, layer in refreshes, and document the employee journey. The best equity plans are predictable, explainable, and resilient to market swings.
